---
name: Recurring commitment edits
description: Product policy for previously edited occurrences when their series is changed.
---

Individually edited commitments retain their individual details when the user later edits the repeating series. Turning repeating off removes occurrence exceptions; the series editor explains this.

**Why:** A single-occurrence reschedule is an intentional exception, such as a changed class or work shift. Silently moving it again with the normal series could make the planner show the wrong commitment time.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in future recurrence controls and clarify any action that discards exceptions. Deleting an occurrence must target its original recurrence identity even after it has moved.
